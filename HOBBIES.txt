HOBBIES & INTERESTS

I have a strong interest in visual exploration and artistic experimentation, particularly in styles such as Victorian aesthetics, minimalism, and dark or atmospheric design. I enjoy creating personal projects that allow me to explore textures, patterns, and conceptual compositions, often inspired by literature, symbolism, and visual storytelling.

I am also interested in digital illustration and generative design, experimenting with tools like Processing to create interactive and data-driven visuals. This allows me to merge design with technology and explore new forms of expression.

Outside of design, I enjoy researching typography, visual culture, and historical art references, which continuously influence and enrich my creative approach. I am particularly drawn to projects that involve narrative, mood, and strong visual identity.
