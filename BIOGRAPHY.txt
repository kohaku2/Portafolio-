BIOGRAPHY

I am a graphic designer with a comprehensive approach that combines creativity, technical skill, and conceptual thinking to develop strong and cohesive visual solutions. My work focuses on visual identity design, editorial design, and the creation of graphic pieces that communicate clearly and effectively, always maintaining a balance between aesthetics and functionality.

I am particularly interested in exploring distinctive visual styles such as Victorian, minimalist, and experimental aesthetics, integrating graphic elements that add personality and narrative to each project. I am proficient in digital design and production tools, as well as creative processes grounded in research, contextual analysis, and strategic conceptualization.

Throughout my experience, I have worked on projects that demand attention to detail, visual consistency, and adaptability across both print and digital formats. My goal is to create pieces that are not only visually compelling but also generate impact and meaningful connections with audiences.

I am constantly learning and evolving, seeking new ways to express ideas through design and to bring value to every project I am part of.
